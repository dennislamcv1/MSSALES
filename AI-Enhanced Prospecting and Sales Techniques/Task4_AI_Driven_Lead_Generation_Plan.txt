SECTION 1: SEGMENTATION CRITERIA (7 Items)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

1. COMPANY SIZE — AI-Generated
   Annual revenue between $25M and $250M and employee headcount
   between 100 and 1,000. This range defines the mid-market
   manufacturing sweet spot where operational complexity is high
   enough to create genuine pain but budget authority is
   concentrated enough for efficient sales cycles.

2. INDUSTRY VERTICAL — AI-Generated
   Primary focus on discrete and process manufacturing sub-sectors
   including industrial components, food and beverage, plastics,
   metal fabrication, and consumer goods production. These
   verticals consistently experience the downtime, supply chain
   visibility, and workflow efficiency challenges that NextPhase
   directly addresses.

3. OPERATIONAL COMPLEXITY SIGNALS — Human-Added
   Companies operating across two or more production facilities
   or managing multi-tier supplier relationships. Multi-site
   operations create coordination gaps that single-facility
   companies do not experience at the same scale, making them
   significantly higher-value targets for NextPhase's platform.

4. TECHNOLOGY MATURITY INDICATOR — Human-Added
   Companies currently using legacy ERP systems (SAP Business
   One, Epicor, Infor, or older Microsoft Dynamics versions)
   without a modern operations intelligence layer. These
   organizations have existing technology investment to protect
   but clear gaps in real-time visibility — positioning
   NextPhase as an additive solution rather than a replacement.

5. HIRING AND JOB POSTING SIGNALS — AI-Generated
   Active job postings for roles such as Operations Analyst,
   Supply Chain Coordinator, Continuous Improvement Manager, or
   Manufacturing Systems Administrator. These postings signal
   that the company is actively trying to solve operational
   problems — often the same problems NextPhase automates.

6. GROWTH OR EXPANSION INDICATORS — Human-Added
   Companies that have announced facility expansions, new
   production line investments, or recent acquisition activity
   within the past 12 months. Growth events create operational
   strain and technology gaps that generate immediate buying
   urgency, making timing of outreach critical and advantageous.

7. GEOGRAPHIC CONCENTRATION — Human-Added
   Initial targeting prioritized within regions where NextPhase
   has existing customer references and implementation team
   coverage: Midwest manufacturing corridor (Ohio, Michigan,
   Indiana, Illinois) and Southeast industrial zones (Tennessee,
   North Carolina, Georgia). Local reference accounts and
   shorter implementation travel reduce both sales cycle length
   and prospect risk perception.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 2: LEAD IDENTIFICATION PLAN (143 words)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

The lead identification workflow begins with AI-assisted database
queries in LinkedIn Sales Navigator and ZoomInfo using the
segmentation criteria above as structured filters. Primary search
keywords include: "manufacturing operations," "supply chain
visibility," "production efficiency," "ERP integration," and
"continuous improvement." Boolean search strings will combine
industry codes (SIC/NAICS for manufacturing), revenue range
filters, and headcount parameters to generate an initial prospect
universe of 80 to 120 companies per campaign cycle.

Each AI-generated lead then passes through a three-step
verification workflow: (1) confirm firmographic accuracy against
the company's LinkedIn profile and official website; (2) scan
for at least one recent trigger event — job posting, news
announcement, or leadership change — that confirms active
operational need; (3) identify a named stakeholder in an
Operations, Supply Chain, or Finance leadership role before
the lead is accepted into the active pipeline. Any lead
failing two or more verification steps is removed or flagged
for future re-evaluation rather than advanced to outreach.

SECTION 3: LEAD PRIORITIZATION FRAMEWORK (6 Items)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

---
TIER CRITERION 1: TRIGGER EVENT RECENCY — Weight: High
AI Role: Copilot and LinkedIn alerts scan for news events,
  job postings, and leadership changes within the past 90 days.
Human Assessment: Evaluate whether the trigger event represents
  a genuine operational pain point or a routine business activity.
  A new VP of Operations hire scores higher than a routine
  facilities maintenance posting.
Score Range: 0–25 points
  21–25: Trigger event directly signals operational technology need
  11–20: Trigger event indicates growth or change — indirect signal
  0–10: No recent trigger event identified

---
TIER CRITERION 2: ICP FIT SCORE — Weight: High
AI Role: ZoomInfo and Sales Navigator firmographic filters
  generate an automated fit score based on revenue, headcount,
  industry vertical, and location matching.
Human Assessment: Overlay the AI fit score with knowledge of
  known buying patterns in that sub-vertical — a metal fabricator
  at $60M revenue may score higher strategically than a food
  manufacturer at $150M if NextPhase's reference accounts skew
  toward fabrication.
Score Range: 0–25 points
  21–25: Matches all 7 segmentation criteria
  11–20: Matches 5–6 criteria with one or two minor gaps
  0–10: Matches fewer than 5 criteria

---
TIER CRITERION 3: STAKEHOLDER ACCESSIBILITY — Weight: Medium
AI Role: LinkedIn Sales Navigator identifies named contacts in
  target roles and flags mutual connections or shared groups
  that lower outreach friction.
Human Assessment: Assess whether a warm introduction path exists
  through existing NextPhase customers, colleagues, or industry
  associations. A reachable VP of Operations through a mutual
  connection outranks an anonymous prospect at a better-fit company.
Score Range: 0–20 points
  16–20: Named stakeholder identified with warm introduction path
  8–15: Named stakeholder identified, cold outreach required
  0–7: No named stakeholder identified in target role

---
TIER CRITERION 4: TECHNOLOGY GAP EVIDENCE — Weight: Medium
AI Role: Copilot scans job postings, press releases, and
  LinkedIn content for references to legacy systems, manual
  processes, or technology upgrade initiatives.
Human Assessment: Interpret the evidence quality — a job posting
  requesting "ERP data migration experience" is stronger evidence
  of a technology gap than a general "process improvement" role.
Score Range: 0–15 points
  12–15: Direct evidence of legacy system limitations or
          active technology evaluation
  6–11: Indirect signals suggesting operational technology gaps
  0–5: No observable technology gap evidence

---
TIER CRITERION 5: COMPETITIVE DISPLACEMENT OPPORTUNITY — Weight: Medium
AI Role: Copilot identifies whether the prospect is currently
  using a known NextPhase competitor and flags accounts where
  competitor contracts are likely approaching renewal windows.
Human Assessment: Apply knowledge of competitor weaknesses most
  relevant to this prospect's profile. A company struggling with
  downtime visibility is a stronger displacement candidate if
  their current vendor is known for poor predictive maintenance
  capabilities.
Score Range: 0–10 points
  8–10: Known competitor in place with identifiable renewal window
  4–7: Competitor suspected but not confirmed
  0–3: No competitive intelligence available

---
TIER CRITERION 6: STRATEGIC ACCOUNT POTENTIAL — Weight: Low/Long-Term
AI Role: Copilot estimates total addressable opportunity based
  on company size, number of facilities, and likely module adoption.
Human Assessment: Evaluate whether the account has multi-year
  expansion potential — could a single-site win grow into an
  enterprise-level relationship? Accounts with clear expansion
  paths receive a strategic bonus score even if near-term fit
  is moderate.
Score Range: 0–5 points
  4–5: Clear multi-site or multi-division expansion potential
  2–3: Limited but possible expansion path
  0–1: Single-site, low expansion likelihood

---
TOTAL SCORING SUMMARY:
  80–100 points → Tier 1: Priority outreach within 48 hours
  55–79 points  → Tier 2: Outreach within current campaign cycle
  30–54 points  → Tier 3: Nurture sequence — monitor for trigger events
  Below 30      → Disqualified: Remove or archive for future review
